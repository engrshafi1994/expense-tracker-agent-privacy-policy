Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Expense Tracker Agent / Shafiul Azam ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

A. Financial Data & Chat Messages

  • Receipts & Expense Logs: When you upload receipts to agentic chat screen or enter expense details in app form, this financial data is processed to help you manage your personal expenses and incomes.
  • AI Prompts & Text Messages: Text inputs, receipt images, or financial queries you submit to interact with the AI assistant are collected to generate responses.
  • SMS Sync: Active only when enabled and SMS permissions are granted. It selectively parses SMS from whitelisted merchants and uses AI to read message content and automatically log transactions. SMS sync runs in the foreground and background; when the app is completely closed, incoming SMS remain stored locally on your device and auto-sync upon your next app launch.
  • Caution: If you receive sensitive SMS messages (such as OTPs or password reset codes) from your whitelisted merchants, we recommend temporarily disabling SMS sync to prevent LLM processing.

B. Device & Technical Identifiers

  • Device Identifiers: We collect Google Advertising ID (GAID), Firebase Installation IDs, IP addresses, and device hardware specifications for security, app authentication, and ad delivery.
  • App Interactions: Information about how you interact with the App (e.g., button taps, feature usage, page views) is collected for performance and analytical purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data for the following specific purposes:

  • App Functionality: To enable AI-assisted personal expense management.
  • Personalization: To tailor financial budgeting suggestions and transaction categorizations based on your past activity.
  • Advertising & Analytics: To display personalized or non-personalized advertisements via Google AdMob and monitor app performance via analytics.
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To authenticate API traffic, prevent unauthorized API abuse, and verify device integrity.

3. Service Providers & Third-Party Sharing

Important Notice: We do NOT sell your financial data, receipts, or chat messages to third parties. Data processed by our backend or API providers is handled under strict service provider terms.

We share necessary data with trusted third-party service providers solely to operate our service:

  • Google Gemini API: Processes your uploaded financial text and prompt inputs to generate AI responses and expense parsing.
  • Google AdMob: Uses device identifiers, approximate location (via IP address), and usage metrics to serve advertisements. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Firebase / Google Play Services: Handles authentication, installation analytics, and backend database security.

4. Data Retention

Your local transaction history remains stored in your device's database until you delete it or clear the app data. Transient API requests sent for AI processing are retained only as long as necessary to complete the functional request and maintain service stability. If you do not enable the Android OS backup feature, your data may be lost upon uninstalling the app or if your device is damaged.

5. Data Ephemerality & Security

We implement commercially reasonable security measures to protect your personal and financial information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Supported Monetary Amounts and Calculation Limitations

Expense Tracker Agent supports individual monetary amounts with up to 11 digits before the decimal point and a maximum of 2 digits after the decimal point.

  • Transaction Amounts: Individual income and expense transactions support up to 11 digits before the decimal point and up to 2 digits after the decimal point.
  • Net Balance: Net balances are calculated from recorded transactions and are not subject to a fixed limit of 11 digits. However, if a calculated balance exceeds the amount range supported by the App's underlying calculation or display mechanisms, the balance may not be displayed or calculated correctly.
  • Amount Limitations: Individual transaction amounts exceeding the supported input limits, or containing more than 2 decimal places, may be rejected by the App.

Please verify your transaction entries, calculated balances, reports, and exported data, particularly when amounts exceed the App's supported range for calculation or display.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us immediately.

8. Your Data Rights & Opt-Out Options

  • Advertising Preferences: You can reset or limit your Google Advertising ID through your Android device settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Data Deletion: You can clear your income and expense logs directly from within the App or by clearing the App's storage via device settings.

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately upon being published on this page.
